Configure XBee

Re-applies the documented client-node radio config. The link will drop briefly while this runs; the page will re-poll once it returns.

python transport/xbee_configure.py \
  --port /dev/ttyUSB0 --baud 115200 \
  --api 1 --rf 1 --router 0 --netid 1234

    


    

      
Rediscover hub

      
Use this if the SPARROW hub's XBee has been replaced with a new chip. The node forgets the saved hub address, finds SPARROW_MASTER again over the air, and pins the new one. Takes ~10–15s; the link drops briefly.

      

      
      

    


    

      
Transport mode

      

        Relay forwards JPGs over XBee to the SPARROW hub.
        Direct uploads straight to the cloud, no radio.
        The launcher picks up the change at the next cycle.
      

      

      

        
        
      

      

    


    

      
Blank frames

      

        Whether captures with no detections are sent. ON = send everything.
        OFF = blanks are dropped on the node (saves airtime; trusts the on-device
        detector). The detector picks up the change at the next cycle.
      

      

        
        
      

      

    


    

      
Audio recording

      

        How the mic (e.g. USB AudioMoth) decides when to record.
        Schedule records a fixed clip every N minutes.
        Threshold records only when sound peaks above a level (VOX).
        The audio service picks up the change at its next cycle.
      

      

      

        
        
      


      

        

          
          
          
Record once every N minutes. audio.SCHEDULE.interval_minutes

        

        

          
          
          
Max 10 s — clips ship over XBee. audio.SCHEDULE.duration_seconds

        

      


      

        

          
          
          
Lower = more sensitive. Records when a 1 s sample peaks above this. Range 1–32767. audio.THRESHOLD

        

        

          
          
          
Max 10 s — clips ship over XBee. audio.RECORD_DURATION

        successful onboarding consumes it and binds it to this device's unit ID.
